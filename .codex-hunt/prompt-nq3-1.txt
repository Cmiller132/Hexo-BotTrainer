## Definitions to implement (state them exactly in the report)
- READ SUPPORT of a certificate: the set of board cells that any
  verifier check touches — window contents at every node's position
  delta, zone cells, quiet/defender edges, budget/role/exposure
  witnesses, D6 image cells. Derive it from the actual verifier code
  (tss_solver.rs / tss_core.rs verification paths), not from intuition;
  document per-check contributions.
- FRAME CELLS: cells in the support that are EMPTY at the root (the
  certificate asserts their emptiness at use time).
- SUPPORT RADIUS: max hex distance from the certificate's root-active
  region (stones placed by the cert's line + root stones it reads).

## Measurements (deterministic seeds, record everything)
1. For every official 19-corpus WIN row at its ladder rung (10k/100k

3. TRANSFER EXPERIMENT (the money measurement): for certs from (1)-(2),
   mutate the root by adding K={1,2,4,8} random legal stone pairs
   OUTSIDE the support (both colors, balanced turn parity, seeded); run
   the strict verifier on the UNCHANGED certificate against the mutated
   root. Report acceptance rate per K. Every rejection: diagnose WHY
   (which check reads outside the claimed support) — each such case
   either shrinks the claimed support definition or refutes the frame
   conjecture; freeze exemplars.
4. ADVERSARIAL: construct positions where distant stones SHOULD matter

   elsewhere) and check whether the verifier's support genuinely

   verifier accepts a cert whose verdict a far stone invalidates,

   everything).
5. Quantify the prospective win: at the 19-corpus rungs, how many TT
   entries/fragments would key-collide (reusable) under support-hashing
