Reading the verdicts. CERTIFIED WIN / CERTIFIED LOSS means the solver produced a certificate that the strict TssVerifier accepted for the exact canonical (symmetry-0) representative — a proof, not an estimate. UNKNOWN means no certificate finished inside this pass's bounds. UNKNOWN is not a draw, a balanced position, or a loss — Hexo has no draw outcome, and CERTIFIED DRAW is reserved in the schema but is never emitted. Shallow openings (the empty root, all 24 first-reply classes, and the entire 3,684-class two-stone layer) are UNKNOWN by design; the certified content is a thin, deep slice of covered human-game prefixes (the final-ply slice of eight decisive corpus games).
