Opening Atlas — certified verdicts explorer

A proven partial atlas of Hexo openings. Each entry is a root position that the solver attempted and an independent strict verifier either certified (WIN / LOSS) or left UNKNOWN. Openings are de-duplicated by D6 symmetry and ranked by human-game frequency (most-played first), so the top of the list is what people actually play — and those most-played openings are still UNKNOWN by design; certified verdicts live deeper in the tree. P0 is the first player (opens at the origin, blue); P1 is second (red). Browse the representatives, replay a certified win through its forced line, or build your own opening and look up its canonical D6 verdict.

Reading the verdicts. CERTIFIED WIN / CERTIFIED LOSS means the solver produced a certificate that the strict TssVerifier accepted for the exact canonical (symmetry-0) representative — a proof, not an estimate. UNKNOWN means no certificate finished inside this pass's bounds. UNKNOWN is not a draw, a balanced position, or a loss — Hexo has no draw outcome, and CERTIFIED DRAW is reserved in the schema but is never emitted. Shallow openings (the empty root, all 24 first-reply classes, and the entire 3,684-class two-stone layer) are UNKNOWN by design. The certified slice is a deep solver-proven corpus (depths —, — certified wins) plus deep human-game endings (— wins and — losses drawn from played games) — — proven verdicts in all.
