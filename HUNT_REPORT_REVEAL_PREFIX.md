remaining reveal-prefix residue.**

On all 19 standard official-profile rows (34 ladder rungs), the sound
`zone_bound` tail is **26,596.504 ms**, only **0.929688x** the historical
**28,607.982 ms** tail. It therefore fails the binding `>=2x` bar in the wrong
direction. Relative to directly timed second-candidate + evaluation + dedup
classification work, the ceiling falls from **12.620% to 11.733%**. On the
R-CD1-comparable pair-generation denominator, after subtracting only the
separately timed offline analysis overhead, it falls from **7.752% to 7.207%**.

The per-row guard also fails: `hu01jk4` falls to 0.778034x historical
(-22.20%) and `mvp2lvc` to 0.770578x (-22.94%). The reordered retained-child
33+ tail grows from **2,460/77,678 (3.167%)** to **7,161/77,678 (9.219%)**.
Thus even though retained winners move into the top four, the classification
prefix does not shorten at the nodes that matter.

The binding bars resolve as follows:

## Counterfactual and exact key

`TSS_REVEAL_PREFIX_STUDY=1` is a new `cfg(test)` observation flag. The corpus
harness requires `TSS_CLOSURE_COUNTERS=1` and aborts if `TSS_ZONE_ORDER` is
anything except off. The key and all bucket totals are written only to
telemetry; no selector, proof number, generator membership test, classifier,
dedup decision, or verifier reads them.

For a turn-start claimant-stone set `S`, the derivation is byte-exact:

```text
d(c) = u16::try_from(min(hex_distance(c, s) for s in S))
       or u16::MAX if the minimum/conversion is absent
zone_bound(first, second) = max(d(first), d(second))
offline key = (zone_bound, historical zero-based ordinal)
```

The historical ordinal is the stable tie-break, exactly as R-OS1 specified.
Retained-child ranks compare `(zone_bound, historical retained-child index)`.
Evaluation-prefix accounting compares every candidate pair, including
classifier rejects, by `(zone_bound, historical evaluation ordinal)`.

The implementation does not retain 1.8 billion candidate records. Each node
accumulates second-candidate, evaluation, and dedup work in exact `u16` zone
buckets. Each retained child stores the historical cumulative point and the
cumulative point within its own bucket. Those values recover the total work
strictly after the eventual winning child in either stable order.

Second-candidate generation happens once per historical first candidate. Its
time is charged to the smallest exact pair-zone emitted by that group, because
the group must be generated by the earliest key it can reveal. An empty group
is conservatively charged to `d(first)`, its zone lower bound. Equal-zone
groups retain historical order. Dedup time is charged at the accepted
candidate where it occurred. This preserves all measured work without
splitting a gate call speculatively.

Only a proven pair Choice node contributes. Recording occurs when its PN first
becomes zero and uses the first child with PN zero, matching R-CD1. Refuted and
unresolved Choice nodes contribute zero prospective saving. The expansion
counter is the number of retained children actually expanded by the unchanged
live search whose offline order key lies after the eventual winner. It is an
upper ceiling, not an observed schedule change.

## Tail-risk finding

R-OS1's retained-prefix signal is real on this paired cohort: retained top-four
coverage rises from 33,386/77,678 (42.98%) to 48,204/77,678 (62.06%). It does
not translate to a sound classifier-work ceiling. The winner's evaluation
prefix grows from 6,315,656 to 17,896,426 candidates (2.83x), avoidable
evaluations fall 5.67%, and avoidable measured time falls 7.03%. The
evaluation-rank 33+ population barely moves (41,551 to 41,323, about 53%),
while the retained-rank 33+ population triples. The fattened retained tail and
the rejected-candidate prefix consume the attractive top-four movement at
exactly the proven nodes.

The expanded-child counter does rise from 176,109 to 201,200. That is only a
counterfactual upper count over the unchanged search's already-expanded
children; it does not rescue the cursor proposition because the binding
classification-time multiplier and row gates fail.
