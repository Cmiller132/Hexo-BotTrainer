## Problem

The strict verifier (packages/hexfield_eq/rust/src/tss_verify.rs — READ it
carefully; it is the normative trusted base) requires every uniform-zone
(defender/universal) node of a WIN certificate to explicitly cover the full
legal reply set. The review-confirmed theorem FHW-T3-R
(PROOF_TSS_ZONES_FHW.md, charge partition section 2.2b; review
PROOF_TSS_ZONES_FHW_REVIEW2.md) licenses restricting defender enumeration
to a computed zone on the D22/RC/WC annotated class — but the certificate
grammar has no evidence fields for the annotations, so zone-restricted
certificates are currently unrepresentable and unverifiable. R-G2-IMPL's
OBSTRUCTIONS analysis is at

## Deliverable: DESIGN_VERIFIER_FHW_EXTENSION.md (worktree root, new file)

A build-ready design for extending the verifier contract, containing:

1. **Grammar extension:** exact new certificate fields for D22/RC/WC
   evidence (annotations, zone description or its recomputation seed,
   digests binding evidence to the node), cert version bump (v2 -> v3),
   backward compatibility (v2 certs verify unchanged).
2. **Acceptance rule, stated precisely:** at an annotated universal node,
   coverage of the computed zone + verifier-side validation of ALL FIVE
   mandatory guards (touched guard, N-touch, direct 1+q<6, WC, N-virgin)
   + annotation validity ==> full-coverage acceptance, justified by
   FHW-T3-R. FAIL-CLOSED everywhere: any guard/annotation failure means
   the node must revert to explicit full coverage or the cert is rejected.
   The verifier RECOMPUTES the zone and guards itself — it never trusts
   finder-supplied sets beyond using them as hints.
3. **Soundness argument:** reduce the rule to FHW-T3-R + G2-Z1 with exact
   theorem-statement citations; enumerate every new trust assumption; show
   the extension cannot weaken verification of any v2/v1 certificate.
4. **Threat model:** how a buggy or adversarial finder could try to abuse
   the extension; why each attempt is caught (fail-closed analysis);
   mutation-test plan (deliberately broken certs that MUST be rejected —
   include the R-Z10 counterexample trace as a mandatory rejection test).
5. **Re-verification protocol:** how existing minted certificates get
   re-verified under the extended contract (P0-P3 precedent), and the
   consistency argument for mixed v2/v3 stores.
6. **Implementation plan:** files, structs, test list, estimated scope;
